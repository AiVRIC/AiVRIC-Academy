Foundations course

GRC Management Fundamentals

Governance, risk and compliance are one system with three questions: who decided, what could go wrong, and can we show it. This course teaches that system the way practitioners run it, from the first policy to the board report.

FoundationsAbout 6 hours8 lessonsFree
Start the course →

What you'll be able to do

  • Explain how governance, risk and compliance fit together, and what breaks when they don't
  • Set up the accountability, policy hierarchy and risk appetite a program runs on
  • Use frameworks and regulations as inputs to one control set, not as separate projects
  • Run a risk register that ends in recorded, owned decisions
  • Design controls, test them and keep evidence that stands up in an audit
  • Run the program on a cadence, with metrics and reporting leadership can act on

The course

01

What GRC is, and why it exists

Three questions, one system: what governance, risk and compliance each answer, who does what across the three lines, and why fragmented programs fail.

35 min 02

Governance: direction, accountability and policy

Charters, committees and decision rights; the policy, standard, procedure and guideline hierarchy; and the risk appetite statement everything else is measured against.

40 min 03

Frameworks, standards and regulations

What ISO 27001, NIST CSF, NIST 800-53, CIS Controls and SOC 2 each are, how laws differ from frameworks, and how one common control set answers many of them.

45 min 04

Risk management: from register to decision

Identifying and assessing risk, inherent and residual risk, the four treatment options, and why every risk should end in a decision someone owns.

50 min 05

Controls: designing what you rely on

Control types and categories, writing a control that can be tested, ownership, and keeping a control baseline honest about what is actually implemented.

40 min 06

Assurance: testing, evidence and audits

Design versus operating effectiveness, what makes evidence audit-ready, how SOC 2 Type I and Type II differ, and handling exceptions and corrective action plans.

45 min 07

Running the program: cadence, metrics and reporting

The annual, quarterly and monthly rhythm of a GRC program, metrics that drive decisions, and reporting a board can hold the program to.

40 min 08

Beyond the walls: third parties, incidents and change

Third-party risk across the vendor lifecycle, how incidents feed back into risk and controls, and keeping a program current as the business changes.

40 min
✦

Course assessment

20 questions across the whole course; 80% to pass. Opens with learner accounts, alongside the course certificate.
